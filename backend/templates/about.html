About SkillNest

SkillNest connects clients who have work to do with freelancers who have the skills to do it. Clients post a project, freelancers send proposals, and the client hires the best fit. When the work is done, the client leaves a review that helps the next client choose.

For clients

Post a project for free, compare proposals, hire, and review your freelancer when the job is complete.

For freelancers

Build a profile with your skills and rate, find open projects, and grow your reputation through reviews.
